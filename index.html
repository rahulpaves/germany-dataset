The problem

Advising one student on Germany means opening fifteen tabs: the programme page for entry requirements, uni-assist to work out whether their board is even recognised, a PDF for the English threshold, and the local German mission's site for a visa process that differs by nationality.

Multiply that by every student asking about Germany, and the research is costing you more hours than the counselling.

Entry requirements

  • General: Recognised upper-secondary school leaving certificate granting higher-education entrance, with strong Mathematics.
  • IB: Higher Level Mathematics required.
  • A-Levels: Must include Mathematics.
  • CBSE / ISC: Science stream with Mathematics, 85%+ aggregate.
  • Other boards: Assessed for country equivalence via uni-assist before TUM will review the file.
  • English: IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88.
  • Selection: TUM aptitude assessment (Eignungsfeststellungsverfahren) — multi-stage and genuinely competitive.

Deadline & how to apply

15 July 2026 Winter semester 2026/27 · international applicants should start ~10 weeks earlier for uni-assist processing
  • Step 1 — Submit certificates to uni-assist for recognition of the foreign qualification (allow 4–6 weeks; this is the step students under-estimate).
  • Step 2 — Create a TUMonline account and complete the online application for the programme.
  • Step 3 — Upload English proficiency evidence and the uni-assist preliminary review documentation (VPD).
  • Step 4 — Sit the TUM aptitude assessment if shortlisted after the document stage.
  • Step 5 — Receive the admission decision (Zulassungsbescheid) — required before any visa step can begin.

Student visa process — non-EU applicant

  1. 1 Admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid) 4–8 weeks after deadline

    Nothing in the visa chain can start without it. Conditional offers are not accepted for the visa file.

  2. 2 Open a blocked account (Sperrkonto) 1–2 weeks

    Proof of financial resources for one year — EUR 11,904 at the 2025/26 rate (EUR 992/month). The figure is revised annually. A parental guarantee (Verpflichtungserklärung) is the main alternative.

  3. 3 Arrange German health insurance 3–7 days

    Public insurer confirmation (TK, AOK or similar) for enrolled students; a travel policy covering the arrival gap is usually also requested.

  4. 4 Book the visa appointment 2–12 weeks wait

    The real bottleneck. Appointment availability at the German mission varies sharply by country and season — book the moment admission lands, not once documents are complete.

  5. 5 Submit the national visa (Type D) application Appointment day

    Passport, admission letter, blocked-account confirmation, insurance, academic transcripts, CV, motivation letter, biometric photos. Fee EUR 75.

  6. 6 Visa processing 6–12 weeks

    Runs via the local Ausländerbehörde of the university city. Build this into the application timeline — it is why a July deadline becomes a tight October start.

  7. 7 Arrive and register (Anmeldung) Within 2 weeks of arrival

    City registration at the Bürgeramt, which requires a signed accommodation confirmation from the landlord.

  8. 8 Convert to a residence permit Before the visa expires

    Apply at the Ausländerbehörde for the student residence permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis), typically issued for two years and renewable.
