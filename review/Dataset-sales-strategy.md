# How the dataset makes money, and what is missing

Written 21 September 2026. Numbers are real: 323 verified programmes plus the
1,638-row UAE register, 21 outreach links sent, 11 opened, 0 paid, burn $5,200
a month.

## The arithmetic that shapes everything

20 founding buyers at $99 is $1,980 a year. Burn is $62,400 a year. Sold one
subscription at a time to solo counsellors, this product cannot pay you. So its
job is not to be the business. It has three jobs, in order:

1. **Prove someone pays.** The first stranger's $99 is worth more to the
   pre-seed conversation than $10,000 of your own time.
2. **Feed the funnel.** Every Germany open is a counsellor identified by name.
   That list is what the CRM, the Academy and everything else sells into later.
   You already said the dataset is probably the wedge. This is the wedge working.
3. **Find the B2B price.** Angela answered $80-149 from a school address. A
   school or agency does not pay $99, it pays $500-1,500 for a licence the whole
   counselling office uses. Ten of those is $10,000+, which starts to matter.

## The strategy, in sequence

### Now to mid-October: convert the warm 30

The 19 emailed today, Kareena's 20 DMs, and whoever the LinkedIn post surfaces.
Target: 5 paid. Not for the $495, for the five names and five quotes.

- Angela first, alone, followed up personally. She named a price. Close her.
- Everyone who opens and does not buy gets one WhatsApp, not another email.
- Every buyer is asked one question at hand-over: "which record did you check
  first?" Their answer is your next LinkedIn post and your first testimonial.

### October to January: sell the season, not the product

You are entering the only months this data is urgent. Numerus fixus closes
15 January. Non-EU deadlines stack from November. A counsellor with a
Netherlands or Germany student has a countdown running.

- One LinkedIn post a week, each a single record: a deadline, a fee, a
  requirement, stated flat with the source. The VU 15-January post writes
  itself. So do TU Delft's 440 places and SUTD's non-existent cutoffs.
- The "which country do you need next?" reply engine. Anyone who names one
  gets: "$99 gets you everything now, and [country] goes to the front of the
  queue." Presales fund the building and tell you what to build. Never build
  a country nobody asked for again.

### November onward: the school licence

The moment a second school address appears in the funnel, add a tier:

- **Counsellor, $99 a year.** What exists today.
- **School or agency, $499 a year.** Every counsellor in the office, an
  invoice they can put through procurement, and one named country built on
  request per year.

Schools pay from budgets, renew by default, and do not comparison-shop a $499
line item. This is where the revenue line lives. The founding-20 scarcity
applies to the $99 tier only, which keeps it honest.

### The UAE file is a different product for a different buyer

It answers "is this institution actually accredited", which no counsellor
outside the Gulf cares about and every Dubai school and parent does. Uses:

- A "check an institution" search on the site, free, as UAE lead capture.
  Ties directly into the KHDA work and Pave's school conversations.
- The revoked-licence story is press-able: 20 institutions, 154 undergraduate
  programmes, licences revoked, several with live websites. Handled carefully
  and factually, that is a story a Gulf education journalist takes, and the
  byline is Pave. Do not run this without deciding you want the attention.

## What you are lacking, honestly

**1. Distribution. This is the one that decides it.**
Your entire reachable market is about 30 people. At the 10-15% conversion a
warm list gets, that is 3-5 sales, ever. Making real money at $99 needs
hundreds of buyers, which needs a reach of thousands. You do not have a
newsletter, an SEO presence, a community seat, or a partner channel. The
weekly record-posts are the cheapest fix; the Germany file ranking for
"study in Germany CBSE requirements" style queries is the durable one; the
counsellor associations and WhatsApp groups Kareena knows are the fastest one.

**2. Proof. Zero customers, zero testimonials, zero named users.**
Everything currently rests on the data speaking for itself. The first five
buyers fix this if you collect the quote at hand-over. Until then, do not
spend on any channel where trust is the constraint (ads, cold email at scale).

**3. Depth where it embarrasses you.**
France is 8 programmes and Switzerland is 3. A buyer who opens Switzerland
first concludes the whole file is thin. Either build both to 25+ or label them
"early" in the picker so the thinness is announced rather than discovered.
And the countries counsellors actually send most students to, the UK, US and
Canada, are absent entirely. That is defensible as a Europe-and-Asia
positioning, but it must be said as positioning, not left as a gap.

**4. Supply chain. Kareena is it.**
One person researches, verifies and re-checks, through an Excel round-trip
that damaged data last week. Before promising "new countries land through the
year" to paying customers, the pipeline needs the send-only-changed-columns
process and a second pair of hands, or the promise breaks in February.

**5. A believable update promise.**
"Your access covers new countries as they arrive" is the subscription's whole
justification for renewing. It needs a public changelog page: dated entries,
what changed, what got re-checked. Costs an hour, converts sceptics, and
forces the update discipline that renewals depend on.

**6. B2B plumbing.**
The token is per-browser and restore is by email, so one paid email can unlock
an unlimited office today. Fine at 20 buyers, a revenue leak at the school
tier. Schools also need invoices, not Stripe checkout. Neither is urgent;
both are blockers for tier two.

**7. A decision about what "make money" means.**
If it means cover burn: this product does not, at any realistic volume, at
$99. The path is 30-50 school licences plus a few hundred solos, which is a
2027 outcome built on distribution you start accumulating now. If it means
prove revenue for the raise and feed the CRM funnel: it can do that by
December with nothing but the plan above. Decide which, because they spend
your week differently: the first says invest in SEO and content, the second
says close the warm 30 and get back to the school contract.
