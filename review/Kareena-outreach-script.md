# LinkedIn outreach, 20 counsellors

One message. Link, price and the ask all in it. This converts lower per
message than a soft opener, and that is the point right now: a clear yes or no
from 20 people is the thing worth having.

## 3. If they go quiet after opening it

Wait four days, then once:

> Did Germany hold up against what you already had? Happy to hear it did not.
> Either way, tell me which country you actually need next and I will put it at
> the front of the queue.

That question gets answered more often than a reminder does, and the answer
tells us what to build.

## Say these, they are all true

- 323 programmes, 105 institutions, 8 countries
- 252 of 323 carry the exact sentence from the source page
- 184 application and visa steps, written as chains rather than a list of links
- Germany is free and needs no signup or email
- $99 a year, and new countries are included as they land
- Cancel from the Stripe billing page, access runs to the end of the paid year

## Do not say these

- Any number of existing customers. There are none yet. This is the first sale.
  If someone asks directly who else uses it, say they would be among the first
  and that is why the price is what it is.
- Any school or agency name as a user or a client.
- Anything about other lists or datasets being wrong. Show what ours says and
  let her compare it to her own notes. Telling a counsellor her sources are bad
  puts her on the defensive; showing her a deadline she did not have does not.
- That the data is complete for a country. Switzerland is 3 programmes and
  France is 8. If someone asks what is thin, say so and offer to prioritise the
  country they care about.
- That it replaces a CRM. It is a dataset.

## Answers to the three questions you will get

**"What happens when the price goes up?"**
Yours does not. $99 holds for as long as you keep the subscription.

**"How often does it change?"**
New countries and programmes land through the year and your access covers them.
The Netherlands was last re-checked on 18 September.

**"What if it is not useful?"**
Cancel from the Stripe billing page. You keep the file until the end of the year
you paid for. Germany stays free either way.

## Facts worth keeping in your pocket

Stated flat, no comparisons.

- **VU Amsterdam Psychology.** Numerus fixus, closes 15 January, 600 places
  across the English and Dutch tracks. Non-EEA fee EUR 14,310 for 2026-27.
- **TU Delft Aerospace.** Numerus fixus, 440 places, closes 15 January at 23:59
  CET via Studielink. A maths and physics level test, an aptitude assessment and
  a selection exam, each with its own date.
- **SUTD Singapore.** Publishes no grade cutoff for any board. Where a programme
  has no published cutoff, ours says so rather than inventing a number.

That last one is the real pitch. Much of the value is in the fields that
honestly read "not published" instead of guessing.

## Practical

- Space the 20 out over three or four days. Twenty near-identical messages in an
  hour gets the account flagged.
- Change the first line for each person. The rest can stay.
- Keep a list of who opened Germany and did not buy. That is the second round
  and it is worth more than the first.
- Tell Rahul the moment someone buys. The first real payment is the one thing
  that has not been tested end to end with a live card.
