## Practical

- Send individually, not as a bcc block. Twenty-one identical emails from a new
  domain is how you land in spam.
- Email A first, all eleven, then wait three days before starting B. If A
  produces a sale you will want to change B.
- Anyone who opens and does not reply within a week gets the WhatsApp line
  rather than a second email.
