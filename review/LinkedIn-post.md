## Notes

**Open with the word Counsellors.** It is a filter. Everyone else scrolls past
and the people you want stop.

**"Tell me the country you need next" is the real engine.** It gets replies from
people not ready to buy, and every reply tells you what to build. Answer each one
with a fact, not a thank you.

**LinkedIn shows link posts to fewer people.** If reach matters more, post it
without the URL and put the link in your own first comment inside a minute.

**Post it the day Kareena starts her twenty DMs.** Someone who has seen the post
replies at a different rate to someone cold.
