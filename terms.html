Who owns this

It is worth being precise about what that covers, because a vague claim is worth less than an exact one. A deadline is a fact. A tuition fee is a fact. Nobody owns a fact, and we do not claim to.

What is owned: the compilation. Which programmes were chosen and which were left out, how the records are arranged, the wording of every note, correction and trap, the application and visa chains written as sequences, and the verification work that stands behind each entry. In the United Kingdom and the European Union that investment also attracts the database right, which protects substantial extraction or re-use of the contents whether or not any individual fact is copyright.

What a subscription lets you do

  • Use the data in your own advising work, with your own students and their families.
  • Quote individual records in a conversation, an email, a counselling report or a shortlist you prepare for a student.

  • Resell it, or build a product or service on top of it, without a written agreement.
  • Use it to train a machine learning model, or scrape it for that purpose.
  • Remove or obscure attribution where you reproduce more than a single record.

If you want to do any of these, the answer is often yes and the conversation is short. Write to rahul@pavetheway.ai.

Each download is traceable

Every response carries a short code derived from the subscription that requested it. It says nothing about who you are, it cannot be turned back into your account, and the same subscription always produces the same code. It exists so that a file appearing somewhere it should not can be traced to the subscription it came from.

On accuracy, which is the other half of the bargain

Records marked as verified were read against the institution's own page on the date shown, and carry the sentence they came from. Records marked compiled were not, and say so on their face. University pages change without notice, so the date on a record is part of the record. Open the source link before you advise a student on anything that carries a cost or a deadline.
