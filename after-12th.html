Studying in Germany after 12th: the 70% rule, and what it actually means

Most pages will tell you the number. Almost none of them tell you what it does, and that is the part that decides whether you can apply at all.

Last checked 10 September 2026 against APS India and DAAD India.

Your Class 12 marksheet is not enough on its own.

German universities work on 13 years of schooling. CBSE, ISC and the state boards give you 12. So a Class XII certificate is not a university entrance qualification in Germany, whatever your percentage. This has always been true and has nothing to do with how well you did.

What changed in March 2026

APS India updated the evaluation criteria for Indian qualifications in the anabin database with effect from 15 March 2026, applying from Winter Semester 2026/27.

"The updated criteria specify a minimum overall score of 70% of the maximum achievable marks in the Indian Class XII certificate."

APS India, announcement of 23 February 2026. Applies regardless of board.

Here is the part that gets misreported everywhere: 70% is a floor, not a door. It does not give you direct admission. It is the minimum you need before either recognised route is open to you at all.

Which means something concrete. A student at 68% who could have used the Studienkolleg route last year is now blocked from it. If that is you or your child, the conversation needs to happen now rather than after an application is rejected.

The three routes that actually exist

Route 1 · most common

One year of a Bachelor's degree in India

Class XII at 70% or above, plus one successful academic year in a Bachelor's programme at a recognised institution. That gives you subject-restricted admission.

Subject-restricted matters. A year of engineering leads to engineering, not to business. Choose the Indian degree with the German one in mind.

Route 2 · needs German

Studienkolleg and the Feststellungsprüfung

A preparatory year, usually two semesters, ending in the Feststellungsprüfung. Also needs Class XII at 70% or above.

It requires German at roughly B2, so it does not suit anyone planning an English-taught degree. Passing the exam also does not give you a place; you still apply separately.

Route 3 · direct

JEE Advanced

A valid JEE Advanced result allows a direct application to a German university for a subject-specific course, without either of the routes above.

JEE Main on its own does not do this. A few universities accept a strong Main score inside their own selection criteria, but that is their rule, not a national one.

One more thing worth knowing: uni-assist decides equivalence for most German universities. Whatever any website tells you, the VPD that uni-assist issues is what actually settles your case.

What it costs, if you get there

€347.10 per semester

That is the full cost of a B.Sc. Engineering degree at Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences, a public university. No tuition fee, only the semester contribution. Taught entirely in English.

This is the part Indian families are rarely shown. German public universities charge no tuition, only a semester contribution of roughly €150 to €350. Private German universities charge €15,000 to €25,000 a year, and most agencies will show you those, because public universities pay no commission.
